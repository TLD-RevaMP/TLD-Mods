The 3D model built into Rossa88.dll (cars/rossa88-parts.glb in the source) is an adaptation of:

  "1988 Ferrari Testarossa" by OUTPISTON
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1988-ferrari-testarossa-1eb0f94889fd4bf8ac05c2fefaf91af9
  Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes: split into the game's car parts (body, doors, lids, bumpers, lamps, mirrors, wheels...), some pieces reduced or
left out (wipers, the flat engine-bay picture), a front trunk tub and a fuel flap disc added, all badges, logos and
lettering removed and their texture areas painted over, textures renamed.
