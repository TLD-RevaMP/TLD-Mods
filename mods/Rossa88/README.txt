Rossa 88 0.1.0 - The Long Drive BETA branch (V2024.11.26b_test), works alongside TLD RevaMP (tested with 0.69.1 dev)

A low, wide late-80s mid-engined sports car with pop-up headlights, side strakes and a full interior.

It is built on the game's own Car07, so it drives, takes damage, wears, is painted, saved and loaded like the game's
cars, and its parts come off and go back on the usual way: doors, front lid (the front trunk is storage), engine lid,
bumpers, mirrors, pop-up headlights, wheels and tyres, fuel flap. The engine, radiator and gearbox are Car07's and sit
in the rear bay. In RevaMP multiplayer every player needs the mod (install it from the mod browser).

Get one
  F1 spawn menu: "Rossa88Full" is the complete car, "Rossa88" the bare shell; each part is in the list too.
  Item ids 108800-108899.

Install by hand
  Put Rossa88.dll in BepInEx\plugins\Rossa88\ (BepInEx 5 for the beta branch).
